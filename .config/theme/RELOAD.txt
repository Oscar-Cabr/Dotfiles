The following are the neccesary commands, instructions to change theme colors:

Waybar
	  pkill waybar
	  waybar &
Kitty
    kitty @ set-colors -a
Hyprland
    (auto)
    (maybe hyprctl reload)

Hyprland palette
	Master palette:  ~/.config/theme/hypr-colors.lua
	Consumed by:     ~/.config/hypr/modules/decoration.lua  (require("hypr-colors"))
	Edit hypr-colors.lua; borders and shadows update on reload.
	NOTE: this replaced hypr-colors.conf when the Hyprland config was
	      migrated to Lua (hyprlang is deprecated since Hyprland 0.55).

Eww
    eww reload
 or
    pkill eww
    eww daemon
    eww open left-bar
Starship
    (auto)
Wofi
    (auto, restart launcher)
fuzzel
    (auto, restart launcher)
Swaync
	pkill -USR1 swaync

Qalculate (GTK)
	pkill qalculate-gtk && qalculate-gtk &

Nemo (GTK)
  pkill nemo && nemo &

The following are modules from where its necessary to change font:

Waybar, Eww (poweroff), fuzzel, wofi
